U-ViT
-----

The diffusion backbone follows U-ViT: All are Worth Words: A ViT Backbone
for Diffusion Models (Fan Bao et al., CVPR 2023). The reference
implementation is at https://github.com/baofff/U-ViT and is licensed
under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 Fan Bao.

The research code lineage contains U-ViT-derived material. The denoiser
implementation in featstaindiff/model.py uses a U-shaped Transformer
design and sinusoidal timestep embedding
from this lineage. The complete upstream copyright and permission notice
is in licenses/U-ViT-LICENSE.txt and is included in source and wheel
distributions.
